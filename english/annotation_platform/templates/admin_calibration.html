  • Majority: the label most people gave for that year (or item); a tie goes to the label listed first (Achieved → Not yet achieved → Moving away from target → Not mentioned). The majority is not the right answer, only a way to see whose standard drifts.
  • Estimate: median (Q1–Q3): the median of everyone's 0–100 estimate for that year, with the first and third quartile; a wide range means people disagree on how sure they are.
  • Mean distance from the group median: this person's estimate minus the group's median for the same year, absolute value, averaged over their years; larger means consistently more optimistic or more pessimistic than the group.
