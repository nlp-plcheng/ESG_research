Study information and consent

Thank you for taking part. We are testing whether an AI system correctly tracks the public commitments made in corporate sustainability (ESG) reports. Your job is to read the AI’s judgements and point out where they are wrong.

What you will do

  • Each item is one public corporate commitment, together with the AI’s verdict on it in each subsequent year’s report, the evidence text it quoted, and a report excerpt (PDF).
  • Check, year by year, whether the AI’s verdict and evidence make sense, and correct the status where it is wrong.
  • For each year, use a 0–100 slider to estimate, from the clues so far, how likely the commitment is to actually be met by the target year.

Data and privacy

  • This study collects no personal data. We record only your anonymous code, your answers and the time taken.
  • Everything you see comes from reports the companies publish by law.
  • The data is used only for academic research and to build a dataset; results are published in aggregate and never identify individuals.
  • You may close the page and leave at any time; the parts you completed have no consequences for you.
