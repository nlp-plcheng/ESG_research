Annotators

Per-annotator statistics → each person's correction rate, source-page verdicts, quartiles of the probability estimates and of the time on task, evidence look-ups, against the team figure.
Results by item → every person's answer to each item side by side (Q0, conclusion, years flagged, minutes); filter to items with disagreement or to one person's items.
Calibration items → the items everyone answers, year by year: majority label, agreement, median and quartile range of the estimates, and how closely each person tracks the majority.
Stars, notes and questions → every star and note people put on items, plus the reasons given for “not a commitment” / “no target year” and corrected wordings, newest first.
